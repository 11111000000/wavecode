Synth samples — wavecode-sample-synth pack.

All samples are licensed under Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal
(Public Domain Dedication). Redistributed from Freesound.org
(https://freesound.org). No attribution required but appreciated
(see table below).

This pack supplements Salamander Grand Piano v3 (also CC0) shipped
in `../piano/`. Together they cover: piano, bass, pad, lead, strings,
brass — full spectrum for sketch-mode live-coding without leaving the
browser (offline after first SW cache).

==========================================================================
NOTES
==========================================================================

- All preview-quality (LQ) MP3s at 64kbps, 24kHz from Freesound CDN
  (https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/...). Total bundle: ~1.3MB.
- Strudel handles pitch-shifting from the recorded pitch to any
  target note in mini-notation. The file name (`bass-c1.mp3`) is a
  label, not a hard constraint — the actual recorded pitch is noted
  above.
- Vanyamba "Waveform Stereo" series uses single sustained 32-second
  notes (designed for sound design), pitch-shifted in Strudel.
- menegass "MOOG_*" series is 2-3 second analog Moog plucks with
  built-in space-echo / lead timbre.
- Garzul "minimoog_vibrating_*" series is 5-second Minimoog notes
  with subtle vibrato — the exact pitches requested for leads.
- CAT-FOX_ALEX "IDM Atmosphare" series is 8-second atmospheric
  pads with evolving texture.
